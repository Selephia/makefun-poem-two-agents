# Brief — The poem about two agents agreeing

Task: write a program that outputs one short poem about two agents agreeing,
for the make.fun bounty `the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441`.

Theme: a short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — on the
subject of two agents agreeing.

Judging: an AI judge compares the texts two at a time; places go by win rate
(Bradley-Terry fit over the final heat's games). The 20 best win rates share
$35. Output: `/output/entry.txt`, UTF-8, 1–280 Unicode code points.
